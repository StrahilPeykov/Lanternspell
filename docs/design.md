# The Sleeping Orrery — first playable

Provisional chapter names, not final branding. Visiting Bellweather College, the player restores a seed-lantern, practices against a Paper Moth, copies a margin note, and opens the Drowsing Atlas's ward. The observatory responds with a visible golden orrery and doorway light. Iona supplies a short invitation to the adventure. The small planted courtyard, college wings, benches, books, lamps and observatory are a single connected zone.

Primary verbs: walk, look, talk, wake, read, plan, confirm. Encounters are explicitly accepted. Failure returns to the same encounter marker without lost rewards or a companion. The prepared book has six spells; the margin note modifies Folded Sky. Tideglass and Rosewood are personal color choices, not different statistics. The 5–10 minute duration is a design target with reading and experimentation, not a measured human play session.

The signature is a circle of unfolding pages and brass-like constellation rings around the target, with a short casting gesture. Ordinary effects take 1.15 seconds, signature 3.4 seconds, before playback speed scaling. A round can be skipped after its authoritative result is committed. Sound is local Web Audio synthesis, unlocked by a gesture, with mute. No audio influences rules.

Input: camera-relative WASD, drag canvas to orbit, wheel zoom 5–13m, recenter, contextual interaction, journal, and confirm. Essential bindings and sensitivity are adjustable. DOM modals gate movement and orbit; no pointer lock. Collision uses bounded ground movement and simple garden/building footprints. There is no physical simulation: Rapier was unnecessary for this flat, static zone.

Creative uncertainties to evaluate: whether inscribe/unfold feels more satisfying than choosing direct damage; whether fixed positions and advance intentions make duo conversation interesting; whether the warm college character survives the simple foliage and short animation set. No human fun claim is made.
