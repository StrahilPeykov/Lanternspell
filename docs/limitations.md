# Benchmark limitations and next evaluation

The chapter is stronger and remains bounded. Human enjoyment and the 5–10 minute target are unmeasured. Runtime art is still simpler than the concept, especially foliage, brushwork, facial character and interiors. The new facades, Atlas and moth are authored GLBs; costume sidecars reuse the same rig. Short clips now blend, but the grand cast remains a slowed/held version of the same licensed gesture. Coarse sleeve/cape intersections remain possible. The camera has no full obstruction solver. There is no new region or interior, voice acting, score, physics expansion or full creator.

Full-book traditions are the provisional normal mode. Automated policies show useful immediate setup and changed priorities, not guaranteed depth or fun. Strong direct damage remains competitive. Defensive Hearth can be slow (14 rounds in one opening policy); the hand experiment sometimes withholds a desired remedy or puts duplicate copies in its four slots. There is no player-edited deck: the eight-page preparation is fixed for this comparison. Both seats retain individual choices and resources but share the chapter’s discovery rewards.

The crane is a provisional visual judgment from runtime motion, not a human preference study. Pages are quieter; the eclipse is less distinctive. Ordinary spells remain brief. All presentations use identical authoritative outcomes. Sound is original layered offline synthesis, not recorded Foley or a musical score; subjective speaker/headphone review remains useful.

Real local duo has completed both encounters with mixed full-book and identical hand traditions, edited joint readiness, skipped presentation and rejoined completed seats. The protocol suite also tests disconnect around commits and actual SQLite process reconstruction. Two-device internet play, real packet loss, deployed eviction and geographic latency remain unverified. No public deployment was performed. Public session creation/retention controls, explicit authorization and actual account Free configuration are still required before publishing.

Solo v1 saves accept additive tradition/hand/fact data and reject malformed new fields. Old saves keep baseline rules; no silent shared/solo merging. No cross-device credential transfer UI or offline install/PWA. The historical repeated-context asset stall was not reproduced in benchmark completion runs; its cause is still unproven.

Performance reports identify Intel UHD/D3D11, exact view/buffer sizes and sampling conditions. Concurrent unrelated browser rendering/recording was observed during late profiling; timings from those windows are not uncontended hardware acceptance results. No general weak-device or 1080p60 claim is made.

Human comparisons next: (1) keep or release Hearth ward against the Atlas, then try Margin’s ward/setup route; (2) replay with seeded pages and decide whether restrictions prompt invention or merely waiting; (3) compare crane versus pages twice from the normal battle camera, including with a friend planning the other action.
