# Visual and technical reference inspection

Inspected 2026-09-10. Proprietary game imagery was viewed on public official pages and was not downloaded into runtime assets.

- [Ni no Kuni II official gallery and gameplay section](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/games/ni-no-kuni-ii): inspected character/interior illustration, built environment imagery and the combat gameplay screenshot. The official gameplay trailer initially loaded slowly, then played successfully. Inspected an excerpt around 25 seconds showing traversal into a rocky arena and a subsequent broad fire effect: the moving character retains a strong cape silhouette, the camera follows without per-step motion, and attack anticipation grows before the large effect. This was a short excerpt, not a full animation study. Useful principles: clothing reads in broad contrasting areas; trim and repeated openings establish scale; foliage frames architecture; detailed ground is comparatively quiet around action. Our camera uses a lower third-person view for traversal, with more overview and upward framing during planning.
- [WitchSpring R official site](https://witchspringr.com/): sought as a secondary reference; browser access was unsuccessful. Not used as verified visual evidence.
